Tesseract reads the text on the receipt ({{ 'installed' if tesseract else 'NOT installed — rebuild the image' }}). DeepSeek (deepseek-flash) looks at the receipt image itself plus that text, and reads the amount, reference and date. Without a key, simple pattern matching is used. A receipt is auto-approved only when the amount is exactly the order total AND Tesseract also sees that amount; an amount read from the image alone goes to review.
